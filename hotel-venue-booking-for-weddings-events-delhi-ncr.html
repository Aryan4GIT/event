Hotel Venue Booking for Weddings & Events in Delhi NCR

A hotel gives you a ballroom or lawn, rooms for out-of-town guests and a kitchen all under one roof. We help you book hotels for weddings, receptions and conferences across Delhi NCR.

Share your date and guest count and we will shortlist hotels that have the space free and fit your budget.

What it suits

  • Weddings and receptions
  • Conferences and dealer meets
  • Ring ceremonies and engagements
  • Guests who need rooms

How to book it

Tell us the date, the guest count and the budget, and we will shortlist venues that fit and arrange the visits.

Because we also run the decor, stage, artists and food, the venue gets planned together with everything else rather than becoming one more thing for you to coordinate.
