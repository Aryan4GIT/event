Cabana & Lawn Seating on Rent for Events in Delhi NCR

Cabanas, floor seating, bolsters and side tables for an outdoor set-up. A cabana gives guests a place to settle in on an open lawn, and gives the lawn something to look at.

We hire them out across Delhi NCR for haldi, mehendi, sangeet and private parties.

Where it works

  • Haldi and mehendi
  • Sangeet lounges
  • Poolside parties
  • Private lawn parties

How to book it

Tell us the venue, the date and the headcount. We quote per item, not per package, so you can hire this on its own or along with anything else on our items on rent list.

Set-up is the day before where the venue allows it, same-day where it does not, and furniture takes a couple of hours.
