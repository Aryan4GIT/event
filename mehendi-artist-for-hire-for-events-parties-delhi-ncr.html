Mehendi Artist on Hire — Bridal & Guest Mehendi

Hire Mehendi Artists for Weddings, Karwa Chauth, Teej and Parties

One bridal pair of hands is four to six hours of work. A function with eighty guests is a queue, and a queue needs the right number of artists or it runs past dinner. We staff both: a senior artist for the bride, and as many guest artists as the guest count needs so nobody waits more than a few minutes. Available in Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Manesar, at homes, farmhouses, banquet halls and offices.

What our mehendi artists do

  • Bridal mehendi — full hands and feet, Rajasthani, Marwari, Arabic, Indo-Arabic, Mughal jaali and portrait work with the couple's faces and names hidden in it.
  • Guest mehendi at the mehendi ceremony, sangeet, haldi and reception — priced per hand or per hour.
  • Karwa Chauth, Teej, Raksha Bandhan and Diwali mehendi stalls, including society and apartment events.
  • Baby shower and godh bharai mehendi.
  • Kids' party and family-day stalls, with quick designs that dry fast.
  • Corporate activations, mall stalls and women's day events.
  • Glitter, white henna and tattoo-style mehendi where the brief asks for it.

Cones, timing and rates

Our artists use organic, chemical-free cones — no PPD, no black henna — so there is no risk of a reaction the day before a wedding. Bridal bookings are quoted per bride and include a trial if you book early enough; guest mehendi is quoted per hour per artist, with a minimum of two or three hours depending on the day. Tell us your guest count and the hours you want covered, and we will tell you how many artists that actually needs.
