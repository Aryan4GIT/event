Wooden Games on Rent in Delhi NCR

Looking for wooden games on rent in Delhi NCR? Wooden games bring a warm, classic look to an event, and they get every age playing: children, parents and grandparents at the same table. SHOWMAQERS delivers them, sets them up and keeps a coordinator nearby to explain the rules and reset the games between turns.

They need no power, make no noise and look good in photographs, which is why they are a favourite at weddings, mehendi and haldi functions, garden parties, farmhouse get-togethers and corporate family days.

Wooden games you can rent

  • Giant Jenga: pull a block, stack it on top, and do not be the one who topples the tower
  • Wooden foosball table: two against two, or one on one
  • Wooden ring toss and hoopla: throw the ring over the pegs
  • Catch the stick: a frame of sticks that drop at random
  • Wooden table and board games for smaller spaces and lounges

Wooden games for weddings, parties and corporate events

At a wedding or mehendi, wooden games keep guests busy between ceremonies. At a birthday or house party they give the adults something to do while the children play. For offices, they suit lobbies and cafeterias where a noisy arcade machine would not. Pair them with carnival games or giant Ludo for a bigger set-up.
