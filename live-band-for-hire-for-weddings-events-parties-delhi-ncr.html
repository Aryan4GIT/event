Hire a Live Band for Your Wedding or Event

Bollywood, Sufi, Rock and Pop Bands in Delhi, Gurgaon and Noida

A live band changes a room in a way a playlist cannot. The singer reads the crowd, the drummer pushes the tempo when the dance floor fills, and the set bends to the moment — the couple's entry, the first toast, the last song. We book live bands for weddings, sangeet and cocktail nights, corporate annual days, product launches, college fests and private parties across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Manesar.

Bands we book

  • Bollywood bands — the songs every generation at a wedding knows the words to.
  • Sufi and Sufi-rock bands for mehfil, sangeet and cocktail evenings.
  • Rock, pop and retro cover bands for corporate nights and college fests.
  • Acoustic duos and trios for dinners, receptions and lounge sets.
  • Punjabi and folk-fusion bands with dhol for the baraat and the after-party.

What to tell us when you book

The date and the venue, whether it is indoors or out, the size of the crowd, how long you want the band to play, and the language and mood of the evening. Most bands play two or three sets with breaks. Many bring their own sound; where they do not, we arrange the stage, sound and lights so the band only has to turn up and play. Share the songs you must hear and the one nobody should play, and the set is built around them.
