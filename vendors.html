What we look for

The roster is vetted, not open.

  • You turn up before call time, every time — that is the whole job.
  • Recent photos and at least one video or reel of you working a real event.
  • A clear rate for each kind of event, so we can quote clients honestly.
  • Your own kit where the role needs it, or tell us what we must arrange.
  • References from two events in the last year.

Questions

Is there a joining fee?

No. Registering and being listed costs nothing. We agree your fee per booking, and the client pays through us.

How long does approval take?

We usually call within a few working days. Busy wedding season can take longer — you can always follow up on +91 99107 72449.

When does my profile go live?

After our team verifies you. Your name and photo then appear in the matching section of the artist roster.

Can I be listed under more than one role?

Yes. Register once and tell us the other roles in the "about your work" box — an anchor who also sings is listed in both.

Can I remove my profile later?

Yes, any time. Message us on the same number and we take your name and photo down.
