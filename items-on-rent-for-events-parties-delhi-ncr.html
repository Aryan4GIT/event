How hiring works

Tell us the venue, the date and the headcount. Those three settle most of it: a 300-guest lawn in May needs a different cooling plan from the same 300 in a banquet hall in December, and the venue decides whether a hanger tent is even possible.

We quote per item, not per package. If the venue already has chairs you like, you hire everything else and keep the chairs.

Set-up is the day before where the venue allows it, same-day where it does not. A German hanger tent takes a day to go up; sound and light take four to six hours; furniture and bar counters a couple of hours.

A technician stays for the event. Sound, lighting and anything with a plug has someone on it from the first guest to the last, which is the part people forget to ask for and then need.

What usually gets forgotten

Power. A hanger tent with chandeliers, a mist fan bank and an LED wall will outrun the venue's sanctioned load, so a generator goes on the list early rather than at 7pm on the day. Ask us for the load sheet with the quote.
