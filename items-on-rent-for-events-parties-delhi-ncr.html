What usually goes on the list

Cooling and heating for the season — tower ACs, coolers, mist fans in the summer and mushroom or blower heaters in the winter. Sound and light: speakers, mixers, mics, par cans, moving heads and the truss to hang them from. Furniture: sofas, cocktail tables, chairs, carpets and the lounge seating that makes a lawn look finished. A bar counter with the glassware. And the structure itself, from a German hanger tent over a lawn to a fabricated stage with wings and steps.

We own most of it, so you deal with one crew and one bill rather than four vendors who have never met.
