How to play beer pong

  1. Two teams of one or two players stand at either end of the table.
  2. Ten cups are racked in a triangle in front of each team.
  3. Teams take turns throwing a ping-pong ball at the other side's cups.
  4. Land a ball in a cup and the other team takes that cup away.
  5. The first team to clear all the other side's cups wins.
