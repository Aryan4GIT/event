Punjabi Dhol for Baraat, Sangeet and Mehendi in Delhi, Gurgaon and Noida

Nothing fills a dance floor like a dhol. One beat and the baraat is moving, the sangeet stage fills up and even the shy uncles are on their feet. We book dhol players for the baraat and ghodi, sangeet, mehendi and haldi, jaago, Lohri, the couple's entry, college fests, corporate annual days and brand launches across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Manesar.

Dhol acts we book

  • A solo dhol player, or two or more for a bigger baraat.
  • Punjabi dhol players in traditional dress.
  • LED dhol that lights up for night baraats and dance floors.
  • Dhol with a DJ — the dhol plays live over the DJ's mix.
  • Dhol with bhangra or giddha dancers for a full Punjabi performance.

What to tell us when you book

The date, the venue and the timings — when the baraat leaves, how far it walks and when it reaches the venue — and how many dhol players you want. Tell us if the dhol should walk with a DJ on wheels for the baraat or play alongside the DJ at the sangeet, and we will line them up so the music never stops.
