Sangeet & Wedding Choreographer on Hire

Book a Choreographer for Sangeet, Annual Day and Corporate Events

A sangeet lives or dies on the twenty minutes the family is on the floor. Our choreographers build those twenty minutes around the people you actually have — the uncle who will not rehearse, the cousins who want a reel, the grandmother who wants one slow song. They come to your home, your office or your society hall, run the rehearsals, cut the music, and stand at the side of the stage on the night. Available in Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Manesar, and pan India for destination weddings.

What a choreographer does for your event

  • Sangeet and mahila sangeet sets — solos, couple songs, family group numbers and the finale.
  • Bride and groom entry, varmala and first-dance choreography, with the cue sheet the DJ needs.
  • Corporate annual day, foundation day and team performances, rehearsed around office hours.
  • School and college annual functions, including large group formations.
  • Flash mobs, proposal setups and surprise performances.
  • Music editing and mash-up cuts, so the track matches the steps rather than the other way round.

How we book it

Tell us the date, how many people are performing and how many songs you want. We quote per package — a set number of rehearsal sessions plus the event day — and send you profiles of choreographers whose style fits, whether that is Bollywood, Punjabi, semi-classical, hip-hop or a mix. Rehearsals usually start three to four weeks out; shorter notice works too, with fewer songs.
