Catch the Stick (Batten) Game on Rent in Delhi NCR

Looking for the catch the stick game on rent in Delhi NCR? Also called the batten game or the falling sticks game, it tests one thing: reflexes. A row of sticks hangs from a frame. They drop one at a time, without warning, and the player has to grab each one before it hits the base.

It takes seconds to learn, a round is over in under a minute, and the crowd watching is half the fun. That is why it is one of the most asked-for games at corporate events, family days, college fests and birthday parties.

How to play catch the stick

  1. The player stands at the frame with a hand ready.
  2. The sticks are let go one by one, in no fixed order.
  3. Every stick caught is a point. The best score of the day takes the prize.

Where the catch the stick game works best

  • Corporate events, offsites and employee engagement days
  • Family days and carnivals
  • College fests and school events
  • Birthday parties for older children and adults
  • Mall and brand promotions, where a quick game draws a crowd

Games to pair with it

Set it up next to catch the striker, the hammer game or punching power game for a reflex-and-strength zone.

Frequently asked questions

What is the catch the stick game?
A reflex game, also called the batten game. Sticks hang from a frame and drop one at a time at random; the player tries to catch as many as possible.
Can it be run as a competition at a corporate event?
Yes. Every player gets the same number of sticks, so scores are easy to compare across teams, and we can run it as a contest with prizes for the top scores.

Yes, for school-age children and up. Our coordinator runs every round and keeps an eye on younger players.
