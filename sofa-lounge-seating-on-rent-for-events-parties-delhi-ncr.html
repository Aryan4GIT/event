Sofas & Lounge Seating on Rent for Events in Delhi NCR

Chesterfields, two and three seaters, ottomans and rugs to sit them on, for the stage, the lounge corner and anywhere guests stop and talk. We hire them out for weddings, private parties and corporate events across Delhi NCR.

Pick the pieces you like and we will bring them. If the venue already has seating you are happy with, you only hire what is missing.

Where it works

  • The couple's stage
  • Lounge corners at receptions
  • VIP seating at corporate events
  • Mehendi and sangeet seating

How to book it

Tell us the venue, the date and the headcount. We quote per item, not per package, so you can hire this on its own or along with anything else on our items on rent list.

Set-up is the day before where the venue allows it, same-day where it does not, and furniture takes a couple of hours.
