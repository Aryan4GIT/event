Jagaran Decorators in Delhi Gurgaon Noida

A jagaran or mata ki chowki runs through the night, and the decoration has to hold up for all of it. Showmaqers sets up the chowki stage, the backdrop behind it, the flower work, the seating and the light and sound, in Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, Ghaziabad and Manesar. We are based in Chattarpur, South Delhi.

What a jagaran set-up takes

A raised chowki with a decorated backdrop for the deity, flower and garland work around it, a clean floor seating area with mattresses and bolsters for the guests, a stage and sound for the singers and the dholak, and warm lighting that stays comfortable through the night. We bring the fabrication, the flowers, the tent and the crew, and we stay on site until it is over.

The same team handles each piece on its own as well — see stage fabrication, flower decoration, tent and pandal setup, sound and light and furniture on rent.

Where we set up

At home, in a society park or community hall, a banquet hall or a farmhouse. Call 9811788643 or 9910772449 with the date, the venue and roughly how many people are expected, and we will quote for it.
