Coconut Water Stall on Rent for Events in Delhi NCR

Something cold and simple for guests on a warm day: a coconut water counter with someone to serve, dressed to suit your function. It works especially well at haldi, mehendi and other day events.

We set it up across Delhi NCR for weddings, birthday parties and corporate events.

Where it works

  • Haldi and mehendi
  • Summer weddings
  • Pool parties
  • Corporate carnivals

How to book it

Tell us the venue, the date and the headcount, and we will quote for the stall on its own or along with anything else from our games and activities list.

Set-up is the day before where the venue allows it, same-day where it does not.
