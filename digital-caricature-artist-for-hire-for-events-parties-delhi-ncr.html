Hire a Digital Caricature Artist for Your Event

Live Caricatures Drawn on a Tablet, in Delhi, Gurgaon and Noida

A digital caricature artist sits with each guest and draws them live on a tablet in a few minutes — a playful, exaggerated portrait the guest watches come together on the screen. The finished drawing can be printed on the spot as a keepsake or sent to the guest's phone to share. We book digital caricature artists for weddings and receptions, sangeet, birthdays, corporate events and family days, exhibitions, brand activations and product launches across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Manesar.

Why digital, not paper

  • Every guest gets a digital copy to share, so the event travels well beyond the room.
  • Prints can carry the couple's names, the event name or a company logo in a custom frame.
  • Drawings can play live on a TV or LED screen, so the queue enjoys the show while it waits.
  • The host can have every drawing as a file after the event — ask when you book.

What to tell us when you book

The date, the venue, how many guests you expect and how many hours you want the artist for; for a big crowd we send two or more artists. Tell us whether you want prints, a branded frame or a screen to show the drawings, and whether it is a wedding, a brand stall or an office party, and we will set it up to match.

Pair it with another stall your guests queue at: a face painter, a tattoo artist or a magic mirror photo booth.
