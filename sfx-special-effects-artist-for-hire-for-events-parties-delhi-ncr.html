Special Effects on Rent for Events & Parties

Cold Pyro, Dry Ice, CO2 Jets, Confetti and Fog for Your Event

Effects are what the phone cameras come out for. Our SFX artists bring the machines, the consumables and the licence to run them, rig them to the stage or the entry path, and fire them on the cue — not two seconds late. Available in Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Manesar, indoors and outdoors, for weddings, corporate events, mall activations and private parties.

Effects we run

  • Cold pyro and sparkler fountains — safe indoors, no heat, the standard stage entry effect.
  • Dry ice and low-lying fog, for the couple entry, the varmala and the first dance.
  • CO2 jets and smoke cannons for DJ drops and product reveals.
  • Confetti blasters, streamer cannons and petal showers.
  • Snow machines, bubble machines and haze for lighting beams.
  • Flame projectors and fire effects, outdoors and with the venue's permission.
  • Smoke-filled entry boxes, reveal curtains and LED-lit smoke walls.

Safety and the venue

Most hotels and banquets in Delhi NCR allow cold pyro and dry ice indoors but not open flame; our artists know which venue allows what and will tell you before you plan a reveal around it. Every job comes with a trained operator, fire extinguishers on the gantry and a rigged cue sheet shared with your DJ and anchor. We do not leave effects to a volunteer with a remote.
