Air Cooler on Rent for Events & Parties in Delhi NCR

High-throw industrial air coolers for open and semi-covered areas, on wheels so they can go where the guests actually are. We supply them for weddings, corporate events and birthday parties across Delhi NCR through the summer months.

Our coolers are for events and outdoor use only. We do not hire them out for home use.

Where it works

  • Wedding and reception lawns
  • Semi-covered marquees
  • Corporate events and fairs
  • Birthday parties

How to book it

Tell us the venue, the date and the headcount. We quote per item, not per package, so you can hire this on its own or along with anything else on our items on rent list.

Set-up is the day before where the venue allows it, same-day where it does not. A technician stays for the event, because anything with a plug needs someone on it from the first guest to the last.

Cooling adds to the venue's power load, so ask us about a generator early rather than on the day.
