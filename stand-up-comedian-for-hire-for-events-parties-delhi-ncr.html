Hire a Stand-up Comedian for Your Event

Clean Comedy for Corporate Events, Weddings and Private Parties

A good comedian gets a room of strangers laughing together inside five minutes, which is why a comedy set works so well after dinner at an annual day, an offsite, a sangeet or a milestone birthday. We book stand-up comedians for events in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Manesar, from TV comedy-show finalists to the comics who work the clubs every weekend.

Formats we book

  • A 30 to 60 minute stand-up set, in Hindi, English or Hinglish.
  • Clean, family-friendly comedy for weddings and corporate audiences.
  • Crowd work, or a roast written around your team or the couple, with your sign-off.
  • Comic hosting — a comedian who also runs the evening as the emcee.
  • Comedy with mimicry, for an act that mixes jokes with Bollywood voices.

Briefing the comedian

Tell us the audience — age, language, how formal the evening is — and anything that should stay off stage. Comedy works best to a seated room with a working mic and the light on the performer, so we place the set in the running order where guests are fed and settled. For an act built on voices and impressions, see our mimicry artists; for someone to host the whole evening, see our emcees and anchors.
