Lohri is an important festival celebrated every year by Hindus and Sikhs. With Lohri being the shortest day of the year, it commemorates the end of the winter season.

Lohri is a celebration of the onset of the harvest season. The festival of Lohri is celebrated in the evening of 13th January every year. During the celebration of lohri, all members of family sit around light fire. The beginning of festival is when all family members from a circle around the fire and start revolving around the fire. This tradition of revolving around the fire is known as ‘Agni ke Phere’. While revolving around the fire all the family members whisper a specific Mantra known as ‘Gayatri Mantra’. With each round they sacrifice a mixture of 5 edible elements. At the end of each round they put the mixture of edible element in the fire by loudly saying ‘Swaha’. The same process is repeated for 7 times.
