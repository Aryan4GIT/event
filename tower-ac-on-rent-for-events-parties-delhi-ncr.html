Tower AC on Rent for Events & Parties in Delhi NCR

A tower AC is a floor-standing air conditioner for spaces the venue's own cooling cannot handle, such as a banquet hall at full capacity or a covered lawn in summer. We supply 2 and 3 ton units for weddings, birthday parties, exhibitions and corporate events across Delhi NCR.

Hot air and humidity can spoil a function in summer. Tell us the size of the space and the headcount, and we will work out how many units you need. For bigger spaces we also supply portable AC and higher-capacity 7.5 and 12.5 ton units.

Where it works

  • Banquet halls and covered lawns
  • Exhibitions and corporate events
  • Wedding functions in summer
  • Birthday and private parties

How to book it

Tell us the venue, the date and the headcount. We quote per item, not per package, so you can hire this on its own or along with anything else on our items on rent list.

Set-up is the day before where the venue allows it, same-day where it does not. A technician stays for the event, because anything with a plug needs someone on it from the first guest to the last.

Cooling adds to the venue's power load, so ask us about a generator early rather than on the day.
