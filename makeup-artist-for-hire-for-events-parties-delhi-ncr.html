Make-up Artist on Hire for Weddings, Parties & Shoots

Book a Make-up Artist for Bridal, Party and Event Looks

A make-up artist at an event is not just the bride's chair. It is the mother of the bride at 6 a.m., the sisters before the sangeet, the anchor before she goes on, and the twelve guests who want a touch-up before the group photo. We send artists who can work that whole list to a clock, with their own kit, at your home, hotel room or venue. Available in Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Manesar.

What we are booked for

  • Bridal make-up — HD, airbrush and natural looks, with draping and hairstyling, plus a trial on request.
  • Family and guest make-up for mehendi, haldi, sangeet, roka, ring ceremony and reception.
  • Party and cocktail looks, and new year or theme-party make-up.
  • Corporate shoots, conferences and award nights — make-up for speakers, anchors and models.
  • Promotional and brand activation crews, including Russian and Indian promoters.
  • Kids' party and mini-salon stalls, where children get to pick and apply safe products themselves.
  • Character, theme and SFX-adjacent make-up for mascots, jokers and costume acts.

Kit, hygiene and timing

Our artists carry their own professional kit and use disposable applicators and sanitised brushes between faces — on a wedding morning with ten people in one room, that matters. Bridal work is quoted per bride and takes two to three hours; family and guest make-up is quoted per face or per hour. Tell us how many people need doing and the time everyone has to be ready, and we will tell you how many artists it takes.
